## Documentation relationship rules

Current-state projections support retrieval and do not become architecture
authority. Historical records remain retrieval evidence. Archive, move, and
directory placement do not alter lifecycle. Supersession is recorded only by an
authorized decision; it is not inferred from document organization.

## Luna stop conditions

Luna stops for a Decision conflict, Gate conflict, Authority conflict,
Lifecycle conflict, missing source, or an insufficient task contract. Luna does
not create a new Gate, alter Human or Project Leader authority, or resolve an
ambiguity by inference.
