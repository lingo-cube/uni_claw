## Classification Contract

Each selected high-value case is classified with the roadmap §7 taxonomy:

```text
Source | Intent | Initial World | Disturbance | Observed Failure | Required Behavior
      | Reality Distinction | Mapped Scenario | Disposition
```

Dispositions: `ATTACH` (pressure maps to an approved/frozen Scenario or the Capstone), `CANDIDATE` (reality pressure registered, no Semantic Gate yet), `RESEARCH` (evidence not sufficient for one bounded Scenario; S1 replay / design queue), `REJECT` (invalid, duplicate, or outside the Runtime direction). **No case remains `UNKNOWN`.**
