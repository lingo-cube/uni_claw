## Scenario coverage

  structured diagnostic stage, and recovery on retry.
- Uncorrelated or missing artifact sessions: existing DSH and Web tests keep
  the requested capability and expose `uncorrelated`, `not-found`, permission,

- Trace comparison: DSH, UniFlow, and UniClaw remain source-native; the
  `traceContext` carries run/session correlation, source authority, observed

not a failed workspace response. Browser verification must use the URL/token
printed by `dsh web`; no token was copied into a public artifact or committed.
